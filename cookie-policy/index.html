Cookie Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

This policy explains whether and which cookies and similar technologies postsignal.eu uses.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files stored in your browser or on your device that let a site remember information about a visitor. Local storage and tracking pixels work in a similar way.

2. What this site uses

The site does not set its own cookies and does not use analytics, advertising tools or tracking pixels. For that reason we do not show a cookie consent banner.

3. External resources

  • Google Fonts. The site’s font is loaded from Google servers. Your browser then sends your IP address and basic technical data to Google.
  • Hosting. The hosting provider may keep standard server logs (for example IP address and request time) for security.

4. Managing cookies

You can delete or block cookies at any time in your browser settings. Since the site does not use its own cookies, this will not affect how it works.

5. Changes

If we add analytics or marketing tools in the future, we will update this policy and ask for your consent before they run.
